# THE-DRESSED-IN-JACKET-POWER-SCALING
💠 PROFILE SUMMARY
the dressed in jacket
The existence of the dressed in jacket (TDIJ) resides in the Unknown dimension—a non-spatial and non-temporal state that transcends the conceptual definitions of space, time, and causality across the entire narrative hierarchy. Within his cosmology, the concepts of space and time are structured as an infinite hierarchical tower (Infinite R>F Transcendence) where each layer regards the space-time concepts below it as mere fiction—and TDIJ stands two tiers beyond the topmost peak of that entire space-time hierarchy system. His strongest moment occurred at school right before graduation, when he felt his power increase far stronger than usual. He once fought an entity ruling over scripts and the infinite narrative hierarchy, and successfully defeated it—and in this battle, it was proven with absolute clarity that the dressed in jacket is immune to all attacks, conceptual erasure, and fate rewriting from the entity the dressed king, placing his abilities and hax two tiers higher than the supreme narrative observer entity. He regards fiction along with author-authority characters weaker than him as mere sheets of paper or spectacles that he can easily scribble over or watch. He is also aware that he exists within a fictional story—even able to access the Replica Internet Archive in the Unknown dimension to scan the existence data and hierarchies of other entities—yet remains unbound by any storyline. His full awareness allows him to freely determine his own path in life without being controlled by any scenario. He is depicted as a human in a blue jacket with star-blue glowing eyes, fighting enemies using star-blue glowing hands, capable of seeing his own future beyond the laws of acausality, and spending his time at school or in a top-floor apartment gazing over a beautiful bluish city bathed in calming and peaceful moonlight.
